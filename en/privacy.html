2. What data we process

Through the contact form: name, phone number, e-mail address, flat address (if you provide it) and your message. Please share only what you want us to know. When you visit the site, the hosting provider may process your IP address and browser technical data in server logs to keep the service secure and reliable.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use the data to answer your enquiry, value your flat or make an offer, and arrange a viewing. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

4. Retention

We keep enquiry data for up to 12 months after the last contact. If we enter into a contract, we keep data for the periods required by law (for example, accounting law).

5. Who receives the data

Enquiries reach us by e-mail (Google, Gmail). We do not sell data or pass it on for marketing. We may share data only where needed to fulfil your enquiry (for example a notary, bank or valuer — with your agreement) or where the law requires. Service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under appropriate safeguards (for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).

6. Third-party content and cookies

This website uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools. When you view the map, your browser loads map imagery from Esri servers, and on the flats-for-sale page it loads photos from the KV.EE portal; those services receive your IP address. Viewing bookings and KV.EE listings open on those providers' sites (Calendly, KV.EE), which have their own privacy policies. Fonts and scripts are served from our own server.

7. Your rights

You may access your data, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Use the contact form on this website or call +372 5563 3416. If you believe we are processing data unlawfully you can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee, info@aki.ee).

8. Changes

We update this policy when needed; the current version is always on this page.
